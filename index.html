Bands follow the TXSE Auction Participation Bands circular (Sep 9, 2026): median(P) ± k × MAD(P) over a 5-minute window, Trade then Quote then Reference Price Method, MPV and basis-point floors, 2.50% maximum. Calibration searches only the permissible ranges and recalibrates weekly on the trailing 30 sessions.
